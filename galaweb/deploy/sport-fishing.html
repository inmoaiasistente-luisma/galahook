New · Multi-day package

8-Day Galápagos Fishing Expedition

8 days · 7 nights · 4 fishing days

Four days offshore chasing striped marlin, tuna, wahoo and mahi-mahi, with a night in Quito or Guayaquil, six nights in San Cristóbal and a day of snorkeling and highlands.

Price$24,999+ taxes per group of 3–4 anglers · one boat

Marlin season peaks November through April.

A day on the boat

  1. 7:00 amYour guide picks you up at the hotel
  2. 7:30 amWe leave port
  3. 5:00 pmBack in port

Lunch of your choice usually fish ceviche, seafood rice or chicken. Tell us about any allergy or food preference.

Snacks fresh fruit, guacamole and chips.

Drinks around 20 local beers, caipirinhas, sodas, Gatorade and water.

Itinerary

  1. Day 1

    Arrival in Quito or Guayaquil. Night at a 5-star hotel.

  2. Day 2

    Flight to San Cristóbal and hotel check-in. Island tour: Interpretation Center, Cerro Tijeretas and Punta Carola beach. Back to the hotel for dinner.

  3. Days 3–6

    Four days of offshore fishing: striped marlin (catch and release), tuna, wahoo and mahi-mahi.

  4. Day 7

    Snorkeling and highlands tour.

  5. Day 8

    Departure.

Fish four days in a row, or two days, a day of snorkeling and highlands, and two more. We plan it with you 20 days before the trip.

What's included

  • 1 night at a 5-star hotel in Quito or Guayaquil
  • 6 hotel nights in San Cristóbal
  • Island tour on arrival day
  • 4 fishing days with conventional gear, bait and crew
  • Lunch on every tour, plus snacks and drinks on board, beer and caipirinhas included
  • 1 day of snorkeling and highlands: shared boat, private for groups of 8 or more
  • One dinner on us with your catch (except marlin) at Restaurante Rosita, over four generations on the island, on the night you choose
  • Airport transfers
  • Bilingual guide throughout the trip

  • Dinners, except the one at Restaurante Rosita
