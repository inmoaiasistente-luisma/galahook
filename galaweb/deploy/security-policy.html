How we protect your information

Keeping your personal and payment details safe is part of how we do business.

Last updated: October 2026

Who is responsible for your data

Galápagos Hook Adventure, a company incorporated in the United States, with its main address at 12515 Lake Square Cir, Orlando, Florida 32821, United States. Our tours operate from Puerto Baquerizo Moreno, San Cristóbal, Galápagos, Ecuador. Email: galahookadventure@outlook.com · Phone / WhatsApp: +1 551-312-9717.

What data we handle and why

  • Bookings and payments. Name, email, phone, date, number of guests, your notes and the amount, plus the documents for your trip (for example tickets or vouchers), kept in private storage. We use them to confirm and manage your booking. Card payments are processed by Stripe: the full card number never passes through or stays on our servers.
  • Passenger details, when your trip needs them. Legal names, date of birth, nationality, gender, travel document type and number, expiry date and issuing country. Optionally, any special assistance, dietary, accessibility or mobility needs and baggage notes you choose to tell us. We use them to organise your trip (flights, lodging, boats and any passenger registrations required) and to look after you safely. Document numbers are stored encrypted, and access to them is restricted and logged.
  • Contact messages. Name, email, phone, what you are interested in, your message, the page you wrote from, your language and your approximate country. We use them to answer you.
  • Site visits. We count visits anonymously: the page viewed, device type, approximate country, the site you came from and a random identifier kept in your browser. We do not store your IP address or your name. We use this to know which pages are useful.

We use your information only for these purposes and to communicate with you about your trip. We do not send promotional emails, and we never sell your information to third parties.

Legal basis

Under Ecuador's Organic Law on Personal Data Protection (LOPDP, art. 7) we process booking, payment, passenger and contact data to carry out your booking and the steps you ask for before it (quotes and questions). The anonymous visit count rests on our legitimate interest in knowing how the site is used.

The optional assistance, dietary, accessibility or mobility details, and the details of minors, are processed only with the express authorization you give by ticking the box in the passenger form (as parent or legal guardian, in the case of minors), and only to organise your trip and look after you during it. You can leave the optional details blank, and you can withdraw your authorization at any time by writing to galahookadventure@outlook.com.

Who helps us process it

  • Stripe — card payments.
  • Supabase — database and private file storage.
  • Vercel — website hosting.
  • Resend — booking confirmations and notices by email.
  • The providers of your trip (airlines, lodging, boats and guides) — only the details each one needs, when your itinerary includes them.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

Galápagos Hook Adventure is a company incorporated in the United States, and Stripe, Supabase, Vercel and Resend are also based there, so your data may be stored or processed outside Ecuador. These transfers are needed to carry out your booking (LOPDP, art. 60).

Third-party content on this site

  • Our typefaces load from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when the page loads.
  • Stripe's payment tool loads on the pages where you can book and may use its own cookies to prevent fraud.
  • The home and sport fishing pages show a FishingBooker booking button. If you book through it, FishingBooker handles that booking under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Links to Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and WhatsApp take you to those platforms, which apply their own policies.

Cookies and browser storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies of our own. We keep a few small items in your browser's local storage: the language you chose, the anonymous visit identifier, and the dates of your first visit and of the last contact reminder we showed you (so we do not repeat it). You can delete them at any time from your browser settings.

How we protect it

The whole site works over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Card details go straight to Stripe. Passenger document numbers are encrypted, trip documents are kept in private storage, and only our team can open the booking panel.

How long we keep it

Only as long as needed for the purpose we collected it for and to meet our legal obligations. After that we delete it or make it anonymous.

Your rights

Under the LOPDP you can ask us to access, correct and update, delete, or stop using your data, to object to its use, to receive it in a portable format, and not to be subject to decisions based only on automated assessments (we do not make such decisions about you). Where you gave us your consent, you can withdraw it. Write to galahookadventure@outlook.com saying which right you want to use; we may ask you to confirm your identity.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can file a complaint with Ecuador's Superintendency of Personal Data Protection (spdp.gob.ec).
