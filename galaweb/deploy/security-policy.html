How we protect your information

Keeping your personal and payment details safe is part of how we do business.

Last updated: July 2026

Information we collect

When you request a quote or book a trip we collect your name, email, phone, travel dates and, for paid bookings, the payment details needed to process the charge.

Payment security

Payments are processed over encrypted connections. We do not store full card numbers on our servers — card data is used only long enough to process your transaction.

How we use your information

We use your information only to manage your reservation and communicate with you about your trip, plus occasional updates if you agree to receive them. We never sell your information to third parties.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data at any time by writing to us.
